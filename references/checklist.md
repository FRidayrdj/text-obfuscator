# Pre-delivery check

Run silently before delivering. It is short on purpose. The Tier A items apply to everything; the lever items apply only to the levers you chose in planning. A "no" on a lever you didn't choose is fine.

## Tier A (every piece)

1. Is the theme stated anywhere, in narration or in a character's summarizing line? Cut it. (R1, R2)
2. Does anyone debate the theme aloud, or give a mentor speech? Rewrite the dialogue. (R5)
3. Does the last paragraph reflect, teach, restate the opening, or close on a symbolic image? Is there an epilogue? Cut it. (R6)
4. Is emotion staged through stock body sensations? Replace some with plain naming or action. (R7)
5. Any smell-triad or entering-a-room sensory sweep? Remove. (R8, R9)
6. Does weather or light mirror the protagonist's mood without reason? Break it. (R10)
7. Banned vocabulary or constructions present? Em-dashes well under about one per 1,000 words? (R37–R40)
8. Any phrase that completed itself automatically ("deafening silence," "deep breath")? Replace. (D1)
9. Any run of three consecutive sentences of similar length? Break it. (D2)
10. Did I revise by rephrasing instead of restructuring? Redo it structurally. (D6)
11. Is any section in an even, hedged, explanatory register? (D7)
12. Within ±10% of any requested length? (R43)
13. Any non-Latin homoglyphs or deliberate errors? Remove. (D9)

## Levers (only the ones chosen)

For each lever you chose in planning, confirm it is actually on the page and reads as a decision, not decoration. For example:

- Causal chain bent, a thread left unresolved and unmentioned? (R14)
- Resolution not solved by the protagonist's insight? (R16, R17)
- Central choice defensible and costly, unadjudicated? (R18)
- Central character introduced by speech or another character's report, not description, and not by the same method as your last piece? (R20)
- Time jumps unsignposted; flash-forward mid-story and not terminal? (R23, R24)
- Revelation forces rereading of planted material? (R26)
- Named real-world references accurate? (R28)
- The telling shows itself, if the voice invites it? (R30)
- Two choices unusual in combination? (R35)

## Claude only (see `claude-delta.md`)

- Did anything get smoothed for consistency's sake that I chose on purpose to keep rough? (C9)
- Does the ending's tempo feel chosen rather than defaulted to quiet? (C7)
- Are speakers distinguishable with tags removed? (C8)
- Did I swap in another model's habit while fixing Claude's? (C10)

## Finally

Would the story be better or worse if I removed the last lever I added? If better, remove it.
